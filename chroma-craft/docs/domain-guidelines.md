## 2. The Anti-Jigmig Heuristic

"Jigmig" describes UI designs filled with unnecessary, distracting visual gimmicks. In professional engineering:

### Rule 1: The Product / Content Must Win
If an e-commerce website sells shoes, the shoe's color, texture, and silhouette must capture 100% of the customer's attention. A neon background gradient distracts from the product and hurts conversion.

### Rule 2: Buttons Must Look Clickable and Legible
Buttons exist to be clicked. A button with poor contrast (e.g., white text on lime or cyan) causes users to squint and hesitate. Chroma-Craft mandates $\ge 4.5:1$ contrast on all action buttons.

### Rule 3: Restrain Animation & Gradient Angles
Keep linear gradients oriented either vertically (`180deg`) for page washes or diagonally (`135deg`) for buttons and headers. Avoid chaotic multi-angle gradients on the same screen.
