## 1. Overview & Isolation Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **STRICT BLUES LIST**: The Blue family is strictly limited to the **7 canonical blues** listed below (`Ice Blue`, `Baby Blue`, `Ocean Blue`, `Midnight Blue`, `Electric Blue`, `Arctic Blue`, and `Steel Blue`). **Do NOT invent, alias, or introduce any other blues** into Chroma-Craft palettes.

## 3. Categorized Color Families

### A. Neutrals & Whites (The Structural Foundation)
Clean readability is non-negotiable in digital design. Chroma-Craft provides 3 distinct whites:
- **Snow White (`#FBFBFD`)**: Ultra-clean, cool white. Ideal for tech, SaaS, modern e-commerce, and high-density dashboards.
- **Ivory White (`#FFFFF0`)**: Subtle warm ivory. Softens optical strain in editorial reading, fashion, and artisanal stores.
- **Pearl White (`#F8F6F0`)**: Balanced organic white. Perfect for secondary content containers and alternating section backgrounds.
- **Charcoal Onyx (`#121214`)**: Deepest neutral surface and typography anchor, achieving a 18.1:1 contrast ratio against Snow White.

### B. The 7 Blues (Strict Set)
- **Ice Blue (`#DDF2FD`)** & **Arctic Blue (`#C6E2FF`)**: Surface tints, card backdrops, subtle glassmorphic glows.
- **Baby Blue (`#89CFF0`)**: Soft tags, inactive tabs, gentle indicators.
- **Ocean Blue (`#0077BE`)** & **Steel Blue (`#4682B4`)**: Authoritative corporate primaries, trustworthy action buttons.
- **Electric Blue (`#0066FF`)**: High-energy CTA, interactive focus rings, modern tech brand primary.
- **Midnight Blue (`#191970`)**: Grounded dark surface, deep header backplates, enterprise anchor.

### C. Greens, Cyans & Earth Accents
- **Teal (`#008080`)** and **Sage Green (`#9CAF88`)**: Reassuring, organic, and calm.
- **Forest Green (`#228B22`)**: The definitive high-conversion action tone for checkout and positive financial telemetry.
- **Terracotta (`#E07A5F`)** and **Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm, inviting editorial accents and high-visibility badges.

### D. Cosmic Ribbon & Luminous Accents (The Spectral Flow)
- **Neon Mint Glow (`#A1EFD8`)**: The vibrant iridescent green/teal rim lining the outer and lower contours. Exceptional 15.06:1 AAA contrast against Cosmic Obsidian.
- **Warm Amber Gold (`#FBBF90`)**: The luminous, incandescent edge lighting on the top-right ribbon. Warm radiant highlight with 12.31:1 AAA contrast on dark canvas.
- **Electric Violet (`#713F91`)**: The mid-tone purple looping through the primary body of the ribbon. Strong brand anchor and button gradient foundation.
- **Spectral Indigo Cyan (`#6B8EBA`)**: The iridescent cool-blue sheen visible across the central and inner curves. Cool transition tint and secondary label.
- **Deep Plum Shadow (`#5D2C5B`)**: The dark magenta/plum shades along the shadowed underside of the loops. Rich wine surface and dark-mode depth layer.
- **Cosmic Obsidian (`#060814`)**: The deep near-black background tinted with subtle midnight blue. Ultimate high-contrast canvas achieving 19.30:1 AAA contrast with white text without OLED smearing.
